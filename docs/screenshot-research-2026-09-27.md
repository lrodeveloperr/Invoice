# Japanese invoice-app screenshot research — 2026-09-27

## Decision

Use three portrait, borderless 6.9-inch iPhone assets at **1320 × 2868 px**. Each asset leads with one oversized Japanese outcome and leaves a large, consistent aperture for authentic final-build UI. The campaign uses dark navy type, a restrained blue/cyan system, generous white space, and no price, rating, ranking, device shell, or unsupported performance claim.

The app's own differentiated story remains the centre of the campaign:

1. Month-end speed.
2. Work dates and job sites converted directly into invoice lines.
3. Exact PDF verification before issue.

## What customers appear to respond to

- **The outcome must be understood before the UI is inspected.** The category leaders use large `請求書` (invoice), `かんたん` (easy), or `スマホ` (smartphone) language at thumbnail size.
- **A finished invoice is stronger proof than an abstract feature list.** Successful creative repeatedly pairs the editing flow with a readable final document.
- **Mobility and speed matter.** Customer praise concentrates on creating a document while away from a PC, handling urgent work, and finishing in minutes.
- **Japanese business specificity builds trust.** The visible invoice, tax treatment, and familiar document structure matter more than lifestyle decoration.
- **Reliability is part of conversion.** Recent negative reviews repeatedly describe freezes, slow loading, or disrupted editing. The campaign should therefore look controlled and evidence-led, not overloaded.

## Creative system

- Canvas: 1320 × 2868 px, portrait, opaque sRGB PNG.
- Caption block: centred, two lines maximum, Noto Sans JP Bold, dark navy.
- Aperture: `x=66`, `y=520`, `width=1188`, `height=1958`, corner radius 64 px.
- Top clear space: 520 px.
- Bottom clear space: 390 px, exactly `round(0.75 × 520)`.
- Backgrounds: pale blue, pale teal, and pale indigo within one campaign palette.
- Authentic UI: inserted later without redrawing; source pixels may only be proportionally scaled and clipped.
- Exclusions: no static purchase price, rating badge, ranking, fabricated UI, English storefront text, or device hardware frame.
