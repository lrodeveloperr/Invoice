# App icon

## Concept

The white document gives immediate invoice recognition. Three connected indigo markers communicate separate work dates being consolidated into one invoice. A restrained vermilion approval mark adds a familiar Japanese document cue without copying another app's logo.

## Palette

| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Deep indigo | `#103F91` |
| Paper | `#FFFFFF` |
| Date connection | `#13B9DA` |
| Approval accent | `#D93B30` |

## Assets

- `dated-service-invoice-icon.svg` is the editable master.
- `AppIcon-1024.png` is the opaque 1024 × 1024 App Store/Xcode asset.
- `icon-size-check.png` shows the artwork at 128, 64 and 48 px on light and dark backgrounds. It is QA evidence only and must not be submitted to the App Store.

The master artwork has a full square background and no baked outer corner radius; Apple applies the device-specific icon mask.
